Why it matters

Landfills weren't built for banquet leftovers.

${''}

Still landfill-bound

Most commercial food waste in India has nowhere structured to go — it ends up in landfill, rotting anaerobically instead of becoming fuel.

Methane, not fuel

Left to rot, that waste releases methane — a far more potent greenhouse gas than CO₂. Routed to a biogas plant, the same waste becomes clean fuel instead.

No proof of disposal

Generators need a verifiable record for audits and sustainability reporting — not a verbal assurance that "someone came and took it."

Guesswork billing

Without a weigh-in at the plant, invoicing is a guess. Food2Fuel bills on the actual weight received — nothing estimated after the fact.

How it works

One request. Four checkpoints. One record.

01

Request a pickup

A generator logs in and requests a pickup. A biogas plant and a delivery partner are routed automatically.

02

Track it live

The delivery partner collects the batch and shares live location until it reaches the plant.

03

Weigh & invoice

The plant weighs the batch on arrival and generates a fair, weight-based invoice on the spot.

04

Pay & get your record

Payment happens in-app. Handover completes only once it's settled — and a digital compliance record is issued.
